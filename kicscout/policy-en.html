01 · Who We Are

KickScout ("we," "our," or "the Service") is an AI-powered football player analysis platform operated by a FIFA-licensed agent. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our Service.

For any privacy-related enquiries, please contact us at ogpitch.real@gmail.com.

※ This policy is written in compliance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). By using the Service, users from all regions — including the EU, UK, US, and Australia — are afforded the same level of protection.
02 · Data We Collect

We collect only what is strictly necessary to provide the Service:

  • Player profile data — name, age, position, height, and weight — used solely to generate analysis reports
  • Email address — optional; used to deliver your report access code and, only if you opt in, our newsletter
  • Movement metrics extracted from video — joint angles, movement speed, balance scores, and similar biomechanical values derived during in-browser analysis
※ Uploaded videos are never transmitted to or stored on our servers. All video processing happens entirely within your browser. Only the extracted numerical metrics are used to generate your report.

05 · Retention Period

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected. Once that purpose is met, data is securely deleted without undue delay.

In practice:

  • Report data — retained for up to 6 months from the date of generation, then permanently deleted
  • Email (newsletter) — retained until you withdraw consent; we will delete it promptly upon request
※ We do not retain data beyond these periods unless compelled to do so by applicable law, in which case the shortest legally required period applies.
06 · Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Limited exceptions apply only in the following circumstances:

  • You have given explicit prior consent
  • We are required to disclose data in response to a lawful request from competent authorities under applicable law, following due legal process in the relevant jurisdiction
※ We do not serve advertising of any kind, and your data is never shared with advertisers or marketing partners.
07 · Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the EU, UK, or any jurisdiction that recognises equivalent rights, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten") — request deletion of your data where no lawful basis for retention remains
  • Restriction of processing — request that we limit how we use your data
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Object to processing — opt out of processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw newsletter or other consent at any time, with no effect on prior lawful processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at ogpitch.real@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days of receiving your request.

If you are unsatisfied with our response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority in your country of residence.
