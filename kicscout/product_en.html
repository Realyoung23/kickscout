FAQ

Common Questions

What kind of video should I upload?
A 30–90 second clip where your full body is visible — training drills, jogging, or match footage. Filmed from the side or slightly diagonal works best.
Is my video stored or shared?
No. Your video is processed entirely in your browser and never uploaded to any server. Only the extracted numerical data is used to generate your report.
How long does the deep report take?
Typically 1–3 minutes. The AI makes 4 sequential API calls to build each section of your report in depth.
Can I access my report later?
Yes. Each report comes with a unique code. Enter it at the Report Lookup page to view your full report again anytime.
