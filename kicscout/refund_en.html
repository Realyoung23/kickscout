Refund & Cancellation Policy

Digital Content — No Refund After Delivery

KickScout AI Analysis Reports are digital content generated instantly upon purchase.

Once report generation has begun following payment, the purchase is considered fulfilled and is not eligible for a refund. This is consistent with standard digital goods policy under international e-commerce regulations, as the content is immediately delivered and consumed.

Eligible for Full Refund

We will issue a full refund in the following circumstances:

• A technical error prevented the report from being generated successfully
• The cancellation was requested before report generation began
• A duplicate payment was charged for the same order
• The service was unavailable for more than 24 hours after payment
How to Request a Refund

Refund Processing Time

Once a refund is approved, it will be processed within 5–10 business days depending on your payment method and financial institution.

Credit/debit card refunds may take additional time to appear on your statement depending on your card issuer's policies.

Chargebacks & Disputes

If you believe you have been charged in error, please contact us before initiating a chargeback with your bank or payment provider. We are committed to resolving any issues fairly and promptly.

Unwarranted chargebacks may result in account suspension from the service.
